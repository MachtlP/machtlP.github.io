- Grew up in Tyrol, Austria, where snow and mountains quietly shaped who I am  
- Pursued a career as an engineer, eventually performing qualification tests on spacecraft  
- Eventually realized my heart was still somewhere between snow and alpine ridgelines
- Shifted back to my roots and earned a Master’s degree in Atmospheric Science in Innsbruck   

Now I find myself in Squamish, Canada, diving into a PhD in Snow Science with the SFU Avalanche Research Program — studying snowpack models and avalanche forecasting, and chasing the same curiosity that started in the mountains years ago.
